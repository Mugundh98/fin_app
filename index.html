Before you trust these numbers

Rates, slabs, surcharge, rebate and cess are set for AY 2026‑27 (FY 2025‑26) from the Income Tax Department's published tables. Verify against incometax.gov.in before relying on any figure. All rates live in one object called RATES at the top of the script — that is the only place to edit when the Budget changes.

This is an estimate for planning, not a filed return, and not tax advice. It does not handle: relief u/s 89, clubbing, set-off and carry-forward of losses, foreign income and foreign tax credit, AMT, capital gains indexation elections, or partial-year residency. Marginal relief on surcharge is computed on total income and may differ by rupees from the department's utility in edge cases.

Before you trust these numbers

Only the sum assured is contractually guaranteed. Reversionary and final additional bonuses are declared by the insurer each year out of its surplus, are not promised in the contract, and can be reduced. That is why the floor scenario above assumes none is ever declared — it is the outcome the policy actually obliges the insurer to deliver.

The return shown is the internal rate of return: the single annual rate at which your premiums, paid when they are actually paid, grow into the maturity amount. It is not the total gain — a policy paying back 1.9 times what went in over twenty years is earning well under 10% a year, because most of the money was in for far less than the full term.

Not handled: surrender and paid-up values, policy loans, riders and their premiums, survival benefits on money-back plans, unit-linked plans, and the tax treatment of premiums and payouts under sections 80C and 10(10D). Mortality charges are not separated out — an endowment bundles cover and savings, and this tool prices the bundle as a whole rather than splitting it.

This is an estimate for planning, not financial advice. It does not name, rank or recommend any insurer or policy, and holds no view on whether you should buy, keep, or surrender anything. Speak to a SEBI-registered investment adviser or an IRDAI-licensed agent before acting on any of it.

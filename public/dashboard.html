Before you trust these numbers

The dashboard shows what you gave it, valued at whatever date your statement is from. It does not fetch live prices, so it is exactly as current as your input and no more.

PDFs are read on a best-effort basis. A PDF is not a table — text is drawn at coordinates, and rows and columns have to be reconstructed from where the words landed. It works on ordinary text-based statements. It cannot read scanned or photographed documents, which contain pictures rather than text, and it cannot open password-protected files: CAS statements from CAMS and KFintech always are, so save an unprotected copy or export a CSV. Always check the holdings it produced before relying on them.

Gain is computed only across holdings that carry an invested figure, and the share of the portfolio that covers is stated alongside — comparing a full market value against a partial cost would invent a profit. Where no class column exists the class is guessed from the name and flagged in amber.

This is a summary for your own reference, not a statement of account and not financial advice. It does not name, rank or recommend any fund, scheme or product, and holds no view on what you should own.

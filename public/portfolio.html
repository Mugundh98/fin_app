Before you trust these numbers

The split is computed from the values you supply, at whatever date those values are from. It does not fetch live prices, so it is only as current as your input. Percentages are shown against the classified total — anything the app could not place is held out and reported separately rather than folded in.

Where no class column is present the class is guessed from the holding's name and flagged in amber. Confirm those before relying on the split: a name-based guess cannot tell an arbitrage fund from a pure equity one, and hybrid or balanced funds are left unclassified on purpose rather than split by guesswork.

The target mix is yours — the app never picks one for you. The listed templates are common textbook splits offered as starting points, not recommendations. Selling to rebalance can realise capital gains and attract exit loads, neither of which is deducted here; the add-new-money route avoids both. This is an estimate for planning, not financial advice, and it does not name, rank or recommend any fund, scheme or product.

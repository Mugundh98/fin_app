Before you trust these numbers

Every figure comes from Screener.in, which builds it from the company's own filings. This page does not check it, restate it, or hold any of it. If Screener is wrong, this is wrong.

It is read by scraping a page built for human eyes. Screener owes nobody notice before changing its markup, and when it does, a section here will go blank — the page names which sections it failed to read rather than showing an empty panel as though it were a zero. Reading a site this way may also sit outside its terms of use; that is a matter between you and them.

Growth is compound annual growth between the two years named, computed on annual columns only — a TTM column is a part year and would flatter it. Where a year is missing or loss-making it is skipped as an endpoint but still counted as a year elapsed, so the rate is not overstated. Margins and gearing are computed from the company's own reported lines.

This is a reading aid, not research and not financial advice. Nothing here is scored, rated or recommended, and no view is expressed on whether any share is worth owning. Consult a SEBI-registered investment adviser before acting on any of it.

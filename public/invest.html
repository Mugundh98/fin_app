Before you trust these numbers

Every figure here is arithmetic on the assumptions shown on the left, all of which you can change. The return is derived from your horizon, not chosen: under 3 years is treated as debt, 3 to 5 as hybrid, 5 to 10 as equity-tilted, and 10 or more as equity. An emergency fund is treated as liquid and assumed not to grow. The three scenarios exist because a single number would read as a promise. Returns are not guaranteed and past behaviour of an asset class does not bind its future.

SIP instalments are modelled at the end of each month, and the annual step-up applies once every twelve months. Annual returns are converted to a monthly rate that compounds back exactly, so 12% a year means 12% a year. Tax on redemption, exit loads, expense ratios and transaction costs are not deducted — your realised corpus will be lower.

This is an estimate for planning, not financial advice. It does not name, rank or recommend any fund, scheme, insurer or product, and it holds no view on which you should buy. Asset-mix labels describe broad asset classes only. Speak to a SEBI-registered investment adviser before acting on any of it.
